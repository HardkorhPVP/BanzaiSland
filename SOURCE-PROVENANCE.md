# Source provenance

BanzaiSland 1.0.0 is a GPLv3 fork of Island 3.7.0 by Devin (subtlegrind), independently maintained and not supported by the upstream Island author. The complete corresponding addon source is in `addons/BanzaiSland/`; original Island license, copyright notices, changelog, documentation, and derived-file headers are preserved there. Upstream baseline: CurseForge project 1734604, file 9461050; archive SHA-256 `47262a842d7f213e1a178ce6dba6b2400a85b232b514aeacc92851c170812113`. See [UPSTREAM.md](UPSTREAM.md), [CHANGES-FROM-ISLAND.md](CHANGES-FROM-ISLAND.md), and [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

## Runtime source and assets

All Lua, TOC, locale, XML, texture, and font files needed by the shipped addon are included in `addons/BanzaiSland/`. BANZAI-owned runtime assets are original class identity artwork, bamboo construction, parchment material, and BANZAI branding. They are separate from the Island upstream assets and are not Blizzard art. Non-runtime design-source manifests and source images are not required to build or run BanzaiSland and are not included.

The shipped display fonts and their terms, sources, and SHA-256 values are documented in `addons/BanzaiSland/Media/Banzai/Fonts/FONT-NOTICES.txt` and [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md). Fonts are included unmodified with the notices supplied for their distribution.
