# Bubbles

**Bubbles** are the small round things beside the island. Each one is a running activity with a time on it - a pull timer, a countdown, a flight, a lockout, a buff you are tracking - and a thin ring that drains as the time runs out.

Bubbles are always dark glass, whatever material the island uses, so the numbers inside them stay light and readable even on Hardkorh Parchment.

## What makes a bubble

| Bubble | Comes from |
|---|---|
| Pull timer / countdown | the game's countdown, DBM and BigWigs pulls, `/countdown` |
| Server shutdown | the shutdown warning |
| Flight | taking a flight path (BanzaiSland learns durations and shows the time left) |
| Mirror timers | breath, fatigue, feign death |
| Battleground queue | the status of your queue |
| Ready-check tally | how many are ready while a check runs |
| Lockouts | instance lockouts you are waiting on |
| Buffs | buffs the game's Cooldown Manager is tracking (as small pills by your unit frame); paladin seals on Forever |
| Navigation | the arrow / distance pill for a waypoint (TomTom, RestedXP, Polestar or the game's own) |
| Retail | Great Vault, Mythic+, Delve and other Retail trackers |
| Extensions | bubbles other addons send through BanzaiSland |

## Reading them

- **The ring** is the time left: full at the start, empty at the end.
- **The number** is minutes:seconds (or a count).
- **Point at a bubble** for its name and detail in a tooltip; **click** to open the related thing where one exists.
- When more is running than there is room for, a **+n chip** appears after the last bubble; click it (or any bubble) to open the **shelf**, a panel listing everything live.

## Pins

A bubble you want to keep in view can be **pinned** (Settings › Bubbles › Pinned bubbles). Pins sit in their own row and do not get pushed out by newer bubbles.

## The warning dot

On the island's other side a small round **warning dot** appears when something needs attention - gear to repair, bags nearly full, mail waiting. Point at it for the detail; click it to act where there is an action.

## The error pill

The game's red error text ("Not enough rage", "Out of range") is shown as a small dark pill that drops out under the island and counts repeats instead of stacking. Turn it off under Settings › General if you prefer the game's own.

## Settings

**Settings › Bubbles** chooses which bubble types show, which side they sit on, pins, and flight and countdown options. **Settings › Activities** covers timers and the buff pills.
