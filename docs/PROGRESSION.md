# Progression

The resting island is your progression bar. It is the one thing BanzaiSland always shows.

## Experience

- The bar fills with your experience; the **level** sits at the left and the **percentage** at the right.
- **Rested** experience shows as a lighter band ahead of your progress; **quest** experience you are about to turn in can show as its own band.
- A **gain** floats onto the bar as *+1,250 XP* when you earn it and the bar eases forward. Quest turn-ins get a **toast** with the quest name and amount.
- At **max level** the bar shows the reputation you are watching, or your chosen alternative (Renown, Warband, honor...).

## Level up

A **level-up card** opens with your new level in the display face, your class's original BANZAI identity artwork, and the things that changed (health, mana, talent points, new spells where the game reports them). A short sound can play (Settings › Sounds).

## Reputation

- Pick a faction to **watch** in the game's reputation pane; the bar shows it when you are at max level or when you choose reputation as the bar's mode.
- Reputation **gains** float onto the bar; a **standing card** shows the faction, the standing and its numbers (*Honored 7,450 / 12,000*).
- **Renown** and **Warband** reputations on Retail show as their own progress.

## Dungeon and raid runs

While you are in an instance BanzaiSland tracks the run - time, kills, experience, money - and shows a **run summary card** when you leave. `/bsl runs` lists recent runs.

## Colours

The bar and the percentage use the **accent** (Banzai, Class or Hardkorh) or a colour you pick under Appearance › More colors. Gains are green on Glass and Solid, deep forest ink on Parchment.

## Settings

- **Appearance** - bar colour, rest and quest colours, idle style (full bar / percentage / dot / hidden).
- **Notifications › Progress** - which progression events appear.
- **General** - how long each kind of card stays.
