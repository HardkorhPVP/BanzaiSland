# Interactive prompts

Some cards ask you something. They carry **buttons**, and the buttons do exactly what the game's own pop-up would do - BanzaiSland presses the game's own secure button for you. Nothing is ever pressed on your behalf, and nothing is ever pressed in combat.

## The prompts

| Prompt | Buttons |
|---|---|
| Ready check | **Ready** · **Not ready** - with a tally bubble counting who has answered |
| Group invite | **Accept** · **Decline** - the inviter's name in their class colour |
| Guild invite | **Accept** · **Decline** |
| Duel | **Accept** · **Decline** |
| Loot roll | **Need** · **Greed** · **Disenchant** (when available) · **Pass** - then who rolled what, and who won |
| Release / resurrect | **Release spirit**, **Accept** a resurrection, return to your corpse |
| Summon | **Accept** · **Decline** |
| Battleground ready | **Enter** · **Leave queue** |
| Dungeon Finder proposal (Retail) | **Accept** · **Decline** |
| Item confirmations | the game's "are you sure" questions, as cards |

## Buttons

- The **first** button is the main answer (green with the Banzai accent, or your class colour with the Class accent).
- The **last** of several is the decline (crimson).
- Anything in between is an alternative (violet) - Greed, Disenchant.
- On **Hardkorh Parchment** the buttons are tones laid over the paper and the labels are written in ink; the hover brightens only as far as the label stays easy to read.
- A greyed button is an answer the game does not allow right now.

## In combat

The game does not let addons press protected buttons during combat. BanzaiSland shows the prompt but its buttons wait; the game's own pop-up still works. The moment combat ends, the buttons are live again.

## Keys and controller (beta)

**Settings › Controls** lets you bind keys to the card's buttons (first, second, third, dismiss) and, with a controller, use the D-pad to pick and A / B to answer - out of combat. Key hints show on the buttons.

## Loot rolls in detail

A roll card shows the item (icon, quality, link) and the buttons. As people roll, their names and rolls line up in their class colours; the winner is announced on the same card. The game's loot roll frames are hidden while BanzaiSland handles the roll (Settings › Notifications › Loot rolls).
