# Notifications

Notifications are the cards BanzaiSland shows on its own, without asking anything of you. **Settings › Notifications** turns each group on or off; **Settings › Sounds** gives each a sound; **Settings › General** sets how long each stays.

## Progress

Experience and reputation gains, quest turn-ins, level-ups, Renown and Warband (Retail), legacy points (Forever). See [PROGRESSION.md](PROGRESSION.md).

## Items

- **Loot** - the item, its quality colour, the count. Rare and better loot can hold longer.
- **Money and currency** - gold, silver and copper picked up; currency gains.
- **Upgrades** - when a looted item is better than what you wear. BanzaiSland checks item level itself and uses **Pawn**, **ItemTree** or **Lootified** when you have them (Settings › Supported Addons › Upgrade checkers).
- **Loot rolls** - see [INTERACTIVE-PROMPTS.md](INTERACTIVE-PROMPTS.md).

## Group and social

Joining or leaving a group, a role assigned, whispers (with **Pigeon** installed), friends coming online, mail waiting.

## World

Entering a **zone** or **subzone** (the card replaces the game's floating zone text), **rare** sightings with **RareScanner**, skill-ups.

## Gear and bags

Low **durability**, bags nearly **full**, **reagents** or **ammo** (Forever) running low, buffs you asked to be reminded of. Thresholds are under Settings › Notifications › Thresholds and reminders.

## Vendor

When you leave a merchant: what you repaired for and what you sold for.

## Dungeons and raids

A **run summary** when you leave an instance (time, kills, experience, money). On Retail: Mythic+ keystone and Great Vault progress, Delves, weekly rewards, work orders, collections and housing updates.

## While you were away

Come back from AFK and a **catch-up card** digests what you missed; Settings › Notifications › Include in catch up chooses what counts.

## The game's own messages

- The game's **information** messages ("You are now AFK") can show as a quiet card.
- The game's **error** text ("Not enough rage") shows as the **error pill** under the island, counting repeats.
- Blizzard's **Talking Head** on Retail can be mirrored as a card with the portrait (Settings › Notifications › Talking Head).

## Do Not Disturb

Turn it on under Settings › General (or with a minimap button) and cards wait in the **history** instead of showing. Scroll over the island to read them later.
